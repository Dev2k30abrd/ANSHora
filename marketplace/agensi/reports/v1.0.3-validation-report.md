## 1. Executive Summary & Release Scope

This final release fix for v1.0.3 implements **only** the two targeted corrections requested:
1. **Currency Parsing & Normalization**:
   - Fixed `clean_number()` to accurately strip ISO codes (`USD`, `EUR`, `INR`, `GBP`, `CAD`, `AUD`, `JPY`, `CNY`, `CHF`) and currency symbols (`$`, `€`, `₹`, `£`, `¥`) with or without whitespace, both prefix and suffix (`USD 100`, `100 USD`, `INR 100`, `₹100`, `EUR 100`, `€100`, etc.).
   - Normalized currency detection so equivalent representations (e.g. `INR 100` and `₹100` -> `INR`; `EUR 100` and `€100` -> `EUR`) are recognized as a single currency rather than falsely triggering mixed-currency blocking.
   - Ambiguous symbols like `$` are handled conservatively and never assumed to mean `USD`. The presence of both `$` and `USD` (or `CAD`) is treated as mixed/ambiguous, blocking cross-currency summation.
   - Genuine mixed currencies (e.g., `USD 100` and `EUR 100`, or `$` and `€`) remain strictly blocked.
2. **Cost-Source Disclosure**:
   - When `direct_profit` is selected and missing cost values are inferred as revenue minus direct profit, `cost_source` discloses `"derived (revenue - profit)"` and tracks `inferred_cost_rows`.
   - The engine never mislabels the entire cost total as directly sourced from the cost column when values are inferred.
   - The existing profit calculation methodology remains completely untouched.

All modifications were strictly confined to `marketplace/agensi/`. The core ANSHora application (`backend4/`, `frontend5/`, databases, credentials) was untouched. No git commits, pushes, merges, or branch changes were made.

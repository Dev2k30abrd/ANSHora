## 2. Critical Defect Resolution: Mixed Profit Derivation

### Problem Identified in v1.0.1:
In v1.0.1, when a dataset contained direct profit on some rows and cost on other rows, `compute_kpis_for_rows()` mixed calculation bases. In the 3-row adversarial test:

```csv
Revenue,Cost,Profit
100,60,40
200,100,
300,,120
```

The v1.0.1 helper reported:
- Total Revenue = 600.0
- Total Cost = 160.0 (60 from row 1 + 100 from row 2)
- Total Profit = 260.0 (direct 40 from row 1 + derived 100 from row 2 + direct 120 from row 3)
- Weighted Gross Margin = 43.33% (260.0 / 600.0)
- Paired Valid Rows = 3

**Why this was flawed**:
1. Row 2 synthesized derived profit ($200 - 100 = 100$) while Rows 1 and 3 used direct profit ($40$ and $120$).
2. Row 2's cost was included in total cost even though its profit was derived, whereas Row 3's cost was missing.
3. It falsely claimed 3 "paired valid rows" when neither direct profit nor derived cost was valid across all 3 rows.

### v1.0.2 Resolution: Single Consistent Profit Policy
Under v1.0.2, the helper implements `select_profit_method()`:
1. It chooses **one consistent policy**: either `"direct_profit"` or `"derived_profit"`.
2. Under `"direct_profit"`:
   - Only rows with valid Revenue AND direct Profit are paired.
   - Row 2 (lacking direct profit) is **excluded** from profit calculation and tracked in `unpaired_revenue_rows = 1`.
   - Paired rows = 2 (Rows 1 and 3 only).
   - Paired Revenue = $100 + 300 = 400.0$.
   - Total Profit = $40 + 120 = 160.0$.
   - **Weighted Margin = $160.0 / 400.0 = 40.00\%$** (Calculated strictly on the exact paired rows!).
   - Reported `paired_valid_rows = 2` (never 3).
   - Disclosed in `analytical_notices`: *"1 rows with revenue lacked supporting data under 'direct_profit' and were excluded from profit/margin calculations."*
3. Under `"derived_profit"` (selectable via `--method derived` or when cost coverage exceeds direct profit):
   - Only rows with valid Revenue AND Cost are paired.
   - Row 3 (lacking cost) is **excluded** and tracked in `unpaired_revenue_rows = 1`.
   - Paired rows = 2 (Rows 1 and 2 only).
   - Paired Revenue = $100 + 200 = 300.0$.
   - Total Cost = $60 + 100 = 160.0$.
   - Total Profit = $(100 - 60) + (200 - 100) = 40 + 100 = 140.0$.
   - **Weighted Margin = $140.0 / 300.0 = 46.67\%$**.
   - Reported `paired_valid_rows = 2`.

## 3. Cost & Profit Consistency & Duplicate Exposure

### Row Exclusion Transparency
- Excluded rows are explicitly reported in dedicated fields:
  - `unpaired_revenue_rows`: Number of rows with valid revenue that could not be paired under the selected profit policy.
  - `unpaired_cost_rows`: Number of rows with valid cost that could not be paired (e.g. missing revenue).
- These fields are published at both the top-level of `financial_kpis` and inside `all_rows` and `deduplicated`.

### Duplicate Exposure Consistency
- `duplicate_exposure` now executes using the exact same profit calculation policy and row-pairing rules as the primary dataset.
- Exact duplicates in both `all_rows` and `deduplicated` share identical calculation methods and profit sources.

## 4. Currency Integrity & Multi-Currency Safeguards

1. **Detection**:
   - Accurately detects currency symbols (`$`, `€`, `₹`, `£`, `¥`) and ISO 4217 3-letter codes (`USD`, `EUR`, `INR`, `GBP`, `CAD`, `AUD`, `JPY`, `CNY`, `CHF`).
2. **Multi-Currency Protection**:
   - If multiple currencies are present in financial columns (e.g., `$100` and `€200`), cross-currency summation without exchange rates is mathematically invalid.
   - Cross-currency totals are automatically blocked:
     - `total_revenue = null`
     - `total_cost = null`
     - `total_profit = null`
     - `average_order_value = null`
     - `aov_type = "blocked_mixed_currencies"`
     - Notice emitted: *"Multiple currencies detected ($ , €). Cross-currency summation blocked without exchange rates."*
3. **Unspecified Currency**:
   - If numeric values lack symbols or ISO codes, `currency_status = "unspecified"`.
   - Analytical notice emitted: *"Currency is unspecified in source data; numerical totals represent raw unit amounts without assumed currency."*
